Immediate questions are: 1) What does test do with arguments passed to it? 2) What, if any, is their later use by shift? 3) How do we get the output of shift?

For the first question, it seems that test is now doing the pin/password check. The two weird strings are subsequently passed to sym.shift which generate the program output, depending on pin failure or pin success. The same shift is performed on both strings, which are de-obfuscated by a Cesar Shift. This is nicely visualized with R2. So now, the mechanism is clear and we can move on to 0x04.
